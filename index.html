Mate's tips

Clean smarter between visits

Tip 1

Clean top to bottom

Dust falls. Start with fans, shelves and the tops of cupboards, then benchtops and tables, and finish with the floors. You'll only clean each surface once.

Tip 2

Microfibre is your best mate

Use it damp, not dripping. Fold it into quarters for 8 clean sides. And skip the fabric softener when you wash it, because softener clogs the fibres.
