Privacy policy

How we look after your details

Last updated 30 October 2026. This policy covers spotlessmate.com.au, our quote and booking forms, and the lead forms we run on Facebook and Instagram.

Who we are

Spotless Mate is a home cleaning service based in Ryde, New South Wales, cleaning homes across Ryde, the lower North Shore and greater Sydney. When this policy says "we", "us" or "our", it means Spotless Mate. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

We only collect what we need to quote, book and carry out your clean:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • About your home: your suburb, the type of clean you want, how often, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and anything you tell us in the notes (for example pets, access instructions or areas that need extra attention). We collect your street address only when we book a clean.
  • Messages: what you send us by phone, text, email, Instagram or Facebook.
  • Website use: how you use our site, described under Analytics below.

We collect this when you fill in the price and booking form on our website, submit a lead form in a Facebook or Instagram ad, or contact us directly. If you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta passes us the details you entered (usually your name, phone number, email and suburb) and also holds a copy under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To work out your price and send you a quote.
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your enquiry and to confirm a time.
  • To send you a confirmation email with the details you gave us.
  • To give our cleaner the details they need to do the job (your name, address, phone number, the clean you booked and your notes).
  • To ask how the clean went and, if you're happy, invite you to leave a review.
  • To let you know about our services or offers. You can opt out at any time by replying "stop" to a text, clicking unsubscribe in an email, or contacting us.
  • To understand which ads and pages bring people to us, so we can improve the site and our advertising.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Who we share it with

We share your details only with the services we need to run the business:

  • Our cleaners, who receive the details for your booking only.
  • Resend, which delivers our enquiry and confirmation emails.
  • Vercel, which hosts our website.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), when you contact us or submit a lead form through those platforms, and for advertising measurement described below.
  • Google and PostHog, for the analytics described below.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia, including in the United States. We also disclose personal information if the law requires us to.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for the following:

  • Google Analytics tells us how many people visit, which pages they view and whether they send a booking request. Google may link this to its advertising services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • PostHog records how visitors use the site, including anonymised session replays that show scrolling and taps. Anything you type into a form field is masked and not recorded in the replay.
  • Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags let us measure whether our ads led to an enquiry and show our ads to people who have visited the site. You can control ad personalisation in your Facebook ad preferences and Google ad settings.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months so we can follow up and quote you again, and booking records for as long as we need them for tax and accounting purposes (generally five years). Session replays are deleted after 30 days. If you ask us to delete your details, we will, unless we have to keep a record by law.

Keeping it safe

Our website uses HTTPS, enquiries are sent by encrypted email, and only Spotless Mate and the cleaner assigned to your job see your booking details. No online service is completely secure, so if you'd rather not send something through the website, you can call or text us instead.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it.
  • You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • You can contact us without giving your name, but we can't quote or book a clean without your contact details.
  • If you have a complaint about how we've handled your information, contact us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
